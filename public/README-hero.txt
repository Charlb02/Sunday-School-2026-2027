Put your header illustration here and name it exactly:

    hero.png

It appears at the top of the registration page, above the cross-and-doves
ornament. Recommended width 800-1200px; anything wider is scaled down.

If no hero.png is present, the page simply skips it and shows the drawn
ornament alone. Nothing breaks.

To use a .jpg instead, change the filename in app/page.tsx (search for
"/hero.png") to match.
